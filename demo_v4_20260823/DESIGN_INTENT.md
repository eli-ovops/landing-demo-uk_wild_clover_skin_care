# Design Intent — Wild Clover Skin Care v4

Created before code: 2026-08-23.

## Design read

Wild Clover is not another booking-platform listing. It is Clover's hands-on facial practice in Shawlands: a client chooses a time, but first needs to recognise a distinct person, treatment pace and place. The original booking surface is practical and monochrome; v4 must feel unmistakably like a standalone therapist brand without inventing any business fact.

## Direction

**Warm vermilion + butter + cobalt appointment journal.** One real treatment photograph sits as a tall framed Hero artefact against vermilion—not as a full-screen black-and-white booking background. The real white logo, exact menu names/prices, Clover identity, Fox Den location and live Ovatu handoff provide business specificity. Large type is capped and used as a compositional element, not as a luxury shortcut.

Page rhythm: `vermilion photo-led threshold → cream choice bands → cobalt treatment sequence → butter public-review proof → ink visit note`. The conversion path remains the existing booking URL only.

## Explicit exclusions

- No black/white full-bleed photo plus white booking card.
- No blue-grey booking-app buttons, repeated package thumbnails, equal service cards or generic spa still life.
- No dark-green/acid, Manrope/DM Mono, left-right split Hero, oversized review metric or dark-close/bright-CTA formula from prior demos.
- No AI imagery, fabricated review, opening hours, credentials, effects, before/after, package or payment claim.

## Asset and closing choice

Five distinct public Wild Clover treatment images and the public logo are used in the smallest necessary range. `CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION: NOT VERIFIED` remains internal in `IMAGE_LEDGER.md`. Closing strategy: **Proof + Specificity + Conversion**—published review evidence, real location and appointment types precede a single external booking action.

## Recognition test

Without the business name, the page still requires replacement of the Wild Clover mark, actual photographed facial work, Clover's therapist identity, The Fox Den/Shawlands, the published techniques, exact live service names/prices, current review record and Ovatu route. It cannot be renamed into a generic spa template.
