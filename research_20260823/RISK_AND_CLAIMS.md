# Risks and claims boundaries — Wild Clover Skin Care

Verification date: 2026-08-23.

| Risk / unknown | Required handling |
| --- | --- |
| No purchase trigger was found | Record `NO CLEAR TRIGGER FOUND`. Do not assert that the business is seeking growth, a new website, more bookings, or a supplier. |
| £600 affordability is not proven | The £65–£100 menu supports only a reasonableness check. Do not infer revenue, profit, budget, payback or purchase probability. |
| Public email is not outreach permission | `[REDACTED_EMAIL]` is a public business email, but deliverability is `UNKNOWN`, decision-maker reach only `LIKELY`, and consent/lawful basis is **NOT VERIFIED**. No contact is authorised. |
| Public assets have no confirmed reuse permission | Keep `CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION: NOT VERIFIED`. Public Instagram/profile imagery, logo and reviews may inform research but are not cleared for copying or publication. |
| Existing booking system is functional | Do not characterise the live site as broken or replace it. The proposed value is an owned editorial landing page and clear external booking path. |
| Existing visual presentation is already polished | A Demo must create a demonstrably better information/trust narrative, not merely restyle an already credible monochrome hero. If that transformation cannot be shown honestly in a 30–60 minute concept, stop rather than manufacture contrast. |
| Health / treatment wording | Do not turn cosmetic-wellness copy into medical claims. Preserve source distinctions and avoid invented efficacy, safety, credentials or outcomes. |
| Location specificity | Do not invent a full street address, parking, studio features or accessibility details; the source intentionally says full address arrives in booking confirmation. |
| Content freshness | Prices, service descriptions, reviews, opening information and social materials must be rechecked immediately before any future Demo or outreach preparation. |
| External-page access | Instagram was publicly readable in this environment; LinkedIn direct access redirected to an authwall. Do not use the LinkedIn result as a hard-gate dependency. |

## Prohibited claims

- “Clover owns Wild Clover Skin Care” — not directly stated in the reviewed live source.
- “The business needs a website” or “this will increase bookings” — unproven.
- “The current website is broken/outdated” — unsupported; it is a functional platform profile.
- “151 five-star reviews” or quoted testimonials — rating and reuse permission not verified.
- Any claim of customer authorisation, email consent, official partnership, client approval, treatment result, professional credential, opening time or exact full address not present in the cited source.
