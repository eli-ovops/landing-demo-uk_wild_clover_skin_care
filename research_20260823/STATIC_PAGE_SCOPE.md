# Static one-page scope — Wild Clover Skin Care

This is a recommendation boundary, not an authorised build specification. Verification date: 2026-08-23.

## Recommended narrative and one CTA

**Narrative:** “A practitioner-led Shawlands facial experience: choose the right holistic facial, understand the approach, then book through the existing system.”

**Single CTA:** `Book a facial` → existing [Ovatu booking page](https://wildcloverskin.book.app/book-now). The page must not promise a booking outcome or impersonate the booking provider.

## Shown in a possible future concept

1. Hero: Wild Clover Skin Care; Shawlands/Glasgow context; verified holistic-facial framing; `Book a facial` external link.
2. Three selected, faithfully condensed treatments drawn from S2—for example Signature, Bespoke and Balance—without medical or result claims.
3. “Choose your facial” comparison using only confirmed duration/price data if rechecked immediately before use, otherwise service names/approach only.
4. Proof/reassurance: fact-level statement that the current profile displays 151 reviews and current 2026 review activity, without copying review text or ratings.
5. Location/contact hand-off and the same external booking CTA.

## Reserved for a final delivery only after customer confirmation

- Final brand copy, accurate current prices, full service list, policies, directions, contact preferences and business hours.
- Customer-approved logo, real imagery, testimonials, review quotations, credentials, practitioner biography and accessibility/medical wording.
- All content/visual rights confirmation, full desktop/mobile QA, domain/hosting, consent/compliance review and any client-approved legal pages.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Rebuilding Ovatu scheduling, availability, accounts, gift cards, vouchers, promo codes, payment or customer login.
- CMS, database, server form, payment, ecommerce, inventory, client portal, live slot widget or persistent content operations.
- Medical diagnosis/treatment promises, invented qualifications, before/after results, fabricated reviews, fake studio/person imagery or inferred operating facts.

## Static-fit gate

**H4 PASS — STATIC SINGLE-PAGE FIT VERIFIED.** The main website opportunity is presentation, service education and an external booking hand-off. The necessary dynamic elements already live in Ovatu and remain external.

**H6 PASS — EXCLUSION CHECK.** No evidence suggests that a new custom platform, complex ecommerce, client portal or ongoing content system is the core requirement. This does not mean an eventual full website cannot have more needs; it only supports the narrow £600 one-page concept hypothesis.
