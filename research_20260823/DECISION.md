# Decision — Wild Clover Skin Care

Verification date: 2026-08-23. This is the research owner’s **SELF-QA** decision, not independent acceptance and not Demo authorisation.

## Verdict: RECOMMEND

**Recommend this business for the next gate: PMO/independent evidence review for a possible £600 polished static one-page Cold Demo.** The recommendation is deliberately narrow: it supports evaluating an owned story-and-booking-handoff concept, not producing, sending, publishing or deploying anything.

## Why it clears the stated bar

1. **Real and visible website value:** current H0 evidence confirms active trading; the official presence is a third-party booking profile whose home hierarchy foregrounds voucher operations while service differentiation sits inside booking. An owned landing page can credibly improve service discovery, trust framing and pre-booking understanding without claiming the existing system fails. Sources: [home](https://wildcloverskin.book.app/), [book](https://wildcloverskin.book.app/book-now).
2. **Static page fits honestly:** the verified solution is informational—practitioner/service story, selected treatments, current trust/location cues and one external Ovatu CTA. It leaves appointments, payment, vouchers and customer accounts untouched.
3. **Sufficient real content basis and plausible £600 economics:** public sources provide exact service language, £65–£100 price points, a public social identity and a live booking destination. £600 is proportionate enough to test commercially (roughly 6–9 listed treatments in gross-price terms), while no revenue or ROI claim is made.
