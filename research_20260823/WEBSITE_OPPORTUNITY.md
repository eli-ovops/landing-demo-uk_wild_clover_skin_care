## Current-presentation observations

Real-browser inspection was performed at 1440×900 and 390×844. Artifacts are listed in `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`.

**Important counter-evidence:** the live platform has a polished monochrome treatment hero, recognisable logo treatment, clear navigation and a working Book CTA. This research does not position Wild Clover as a visibly poor or broken website. The candidate value is the absence of an owned, more editorial service-and-trust layer—not a cosmetic rescue.

1. **Home-page hierarchy starts with gift-voucher operational instructions**, including physical/e-voucher handling and promo-code caveat, before the visitor sees a short service proposition. Source: [S1](https://wildcloverskin.book.app/). This is a reproducible hierarchy observation, not criticism of the booking system.
2. **The business’s distinctive services and prices are inside the booking sequence.** A visitor must enter `/book-now` to read the Signature, Bespoke, Balance, Flow and Nurture descriptions and prices. Source: [S2](https://wildcloverskin.book.app/book-now).
3. **Trust and location are present but dispersed.** The home page has 151 reviews, a current review, location, email and social links, yet no owned editorial page that brings the practitioner/service story, treatment choice and proof together before booking. Source: [S1](https://wildcloverskin.book.app/).
4. **Mobile remains functional but is platform-led.** At 390px, the booking cards remain readable and the mobile menu provides the booking sections. The observed opportunity is not a claim that mobile is broken; it is that the same platform-led hierarchy persists on a small screen.

## Why an owned one-page site has plausible commercial value

The business sells £65–£100 individual treatments on S2. A £600 one-time page equals roughly six £100 Signature facials or nine £65 Flow facials in gross sales, before costs; this is only an order-of-magnitude reasonableness check, **not** a claim about revenue, payback or purchase likelihood. The stronger value case is a durable owned explanation/trust layer before the existing booking destination—not a replacement system.
