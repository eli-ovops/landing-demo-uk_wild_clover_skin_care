## Design read

Reading this as: **a local independent facial therapist’s appointment page for a client who first needs to feel the quality of the treatment, then choose a real appointment**, with a quiet photography-editorial language rather than a luxury campaign, generic wellness landing page or online-shop catalogue.

`DESIGN_VARIANCE: 6` · `MOTION_INTENSITY: 1` · `VISUAL_DENSITY: 5`

## First-principles design decision

The customer is not buying a generic “calm feeling”; they are deciding whether Clover’s hands-on care, time commitment and Shawlands setting are right for them. Therefore the real treatment work—not large type, generic texture or coloured cards—must carry the primary visual weight. The page only needs one conversion action: continue to the existing Ovatu availability.

## Approved page architecture

1. **Photographic entrance:** one full real treatment image establishes the physical work. No text overlay, collage or oversized display claim.
2. **Appointment note:** a restrained, central statement identifies Clover / Fox Den / Shawlands and provides the only immediate booking action.
3. **Current appointments:** three compact, factual appointment bookmarks (name, duration, current price), not cards or product listings.
4. **Treatment editorial:** the four distinct official public `/pass` photographs become an asymmetric photographic spread. It carries no packages, discount, expiry or purchase language.
5. **Practice / place:** one concise published-method note and a local booking handoff.

## Visual rules

- Warm bone paper, charcoal ink and a muted slate-blue action tone sampled from the live booking environment; no acid green, purple gradient, deep-green template system or repeated coloured panels.
- Libre Bodoni is used only for small-to-medium editorial headings; Public Sans holds all booking information. Main display type caps at 42px desktop and 36px mobile.
- Maximum one uppercase eyebrow in the entire visible page (the Concept Preview identity label is exempt). No split headline + explanatory paragraph headers; no equal-card grids; no stat callout.
- Every displayed photograph is a distinct, ledgered real Wild Clover public image and serves one placement only. The generated botanical image remains local but is not rendered.

## Recognition test

Without the name, the real logo, real treatment sequence, Clover’s stated facial-therapist role, Fox Den / Shawlands, exact current appointments and Ovatu route must all be replaced. This cannot be re-labelled as a generic spa template.

> **AUTHORITATIVE OPTIMISATION DIRECTION — 2026-08-23.** The preceding v3 rework direction remains a recorded attempt, but is superseded by this decision-led revision following direct user feedback and a Chrome review of Glasgow facial-treatment competitors.

## The correction

The page was still acting like a composed campaign image: photo, floating card and a decorative three-colour treatment layout. That obscured the actual reason to book. The revision is an **independent practitioner’s appointment guide**: a calm, factual invitation that lets a visitor recognise Clover, understand three real starting points, then take the existing Ovatu route.

## Visual system

- **Not a Hero:** the real treatment photograph is a framed treatment scene following a concise introduction. It carries physical context without carrying an oversized slogan or text overlay.
- **Material language:** warm paper, olive ink, faded celery and a restrained rust rule. This is neither the rejected deep-green/acid system nor the former lilac/rose editorial campaign palette.
- **Typography:** Sora for firm, modest headings and Figtree for clear appointment information. No Manrope, DM Mono, Newsreader or oversized serif display.
- **Decision rhythm:** practitioner introduction → real treatment scene → a vertically ordered appointment list → published hands-on approach → Fox Den / Ovatu handoff. A visitor is never asked to decode a moodboard before finding an appointment.
- **Interaction:** no decorative motion. Links retain visible keyboard focus and touch-sized targets.
- **Additional imagery:** four further public Wild Clover treatment photographs from the current official `/pass` page form a distinct post-appointment treatment sequence. They retain their original business context but do not reproduce that page’s packages, purchase controls, discount wording or card UI. The former botanical/linen concept image is no longer rendered.

## Competitor learning applied without copying

The public Glasgow facial-treatment market makes duration, price, location and one booking path easy to find. Facespace puts the people and tactile practice first; 33 Dowanhill gives a bespoke-treatment rationale; PURE makes location and booking logistics explicit. This revision borrows only those information-design principles. It copies no brand expression, image, layout, review, copy or treatment claim.

## Recognition test

Without the merchant name, the real Wild Clover logo and treatment photograph, Clover’s publicly stated role, the three exact appointment names / durations / prices, The Fox Den in Shawlands, published methods and Ovatu route are still needed to replace the page. It therefore cannot be re-labelled as a generic salon page.

## Rework design read

Reading this as: a personal facial-therapy booking page for someone deciding how they want to feel after a session, with a soft, lived-in treatment-journal language rather than a salon landing page or service-selector interface.

The independent review found the preceding system too close to Lavella: `Manrope + DM Mono`, deep green/acid yellow, split Hero, three compact choice rows, standalone review figure and dark CTA close. None of those devices are retained.

## New visual direction

- **One photographic threshold:** the current official treatment photograph fills the initial frame, with a small overlapping factual note. It is neither a left/right Hero nor reused.
- **An intimate paper palette:** mineral grey, faded lilac, muted rose and soft ink. No deep-green/fluorescent-yellow system.
- **Typography:** Newsreader for modest treatment names and Albert Sans for practical detail. No Manrope or DM Mono. Display type caps at 42px desktop.
- **Layout:** treatment moment → choose-your-pace path → Clover’s published hands-on approach → Shawlands/Fox Den handoff.
- **Motion:** one GSAP entrance, 200–300ms, only to establish hierarchy and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`; no looping decoration.
